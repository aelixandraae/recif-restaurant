(About)

RÉCIF is built into the rock: a ring of glass above the water, lit by candle and hearth. We cook what the boats bring into Ajaccio that morning, and nothing on the plate travels further than the bay you can see from your table.

A table on the edge of the reef

(What we cook by) 01 / 04
Tide Stone Fire Salt

Tide

The menu follows the water. Fish landed at dawn in Ajaccio, chalked on the board by noon, gone by evening.

Stone

Built into the rock, not on top of it. The dining room curves with the cliff so every table faces the sea.

Fire

An open hearth of olive and holm-oak wood. We cook over flame, simply, and let the fish speak for itself.

Salt

Oysters from the Diana lagoon, wild fennel and myrtle from the maquis, and sea salt dried on our own terrace.

Who we are
